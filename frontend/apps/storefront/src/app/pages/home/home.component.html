From Ethiopian highlands
to the world's table.

Gadaa Istar Business Group S.C. is your direct link to Ethiopia's finest agricultural and mineral exports. We source single-origin green coffee from Sidamo, Yirgacheffe, Harrar, and Guji; golden sesame and Niger seed from the highland farms; wild highland honey; hand-blended berbere, mitmita, and korerima spices; teff and injera flour; and ethically mined gold and gemstones. Every shipment is traceable to its origin, quality-checked at our Addis Ababa facility, and delivered with compliant export documentation.
