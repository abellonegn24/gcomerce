Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

At Gadaa Istar, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website and purchase Ethiopian coffee and specialty exports from us.

1. Information We Collect

We collect several types of information to provide you with the best shopping experience and to process your orders efficiently.

Personal Information

When you create an account or place an order, we collect:

  • Name and Contact Details: Full name, email address, phone number, and mailing address for shipping and billing purposes.
  • Account Information: Username, password (encrypted), and account preferences.
  • Payment Information: Payment method details processed through secure payment gateways. We do not store complete credit card numbers on our servers.
  • Shipping Information: Delivery addresses, special delivery instructions, and recipient information for gifts.

Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, we automatically collect certain information through cookies and similar technologies:

  • Device Information: IP address, browser type, operating system, and device identifiers.
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent on pages, click patterns, and browsing history.
  • Location Data: Approximate geographic location based on IP address for shipping and tax calculation purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for various purposes related to our business operations and to enhance your shopping experience.

Order Processing and Fulfillment

Your personal information is essential for:

  • Processing and confirming your orders for Ethiopian coffee and specialty products
  • Arranging shipping and delivery of your purchases
  • Providing order status updates and tracking information
  • Handling returns, refunds, and customer service inquiries
  • Communicating about your orders via email or phone

Account Management

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Authenticate your identity when you log in
  • Process password reset requests
  • Store your shipping addresses for future orders
  • Maintain your order history and preferences

Communication and Marketing

With your consent, we may use your information to:

  • Send newsletters about new coffee arrivals, seasonal offerings, and special promotions
  • Provide personalized product recommendations based on your purchase history
  • Share information about Ethiopian coffee culture, brewing tips, and educational content

You can opt out of marketing communications at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in our emails or updating your account preferences.

Security and Fraud Prevention

We use your information to:

  • Detect and prevent fraudulent transactions
  • Verify your identity for security purposes
  • Comply with legal obligations and export regulations
  • Protect our website, services, and users from abuse

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We respect your privacy and do not sell your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in specific circumstances described below.

Service Providers

We work with trusted third-party service providers who help us operate our business:

  • Payment Processors: Mastercard Payment Gateway Services (MPGS) and CyberSource for secure payment processing
  • Shipping Carriers: International shipping companies to deliver your orders
  • Email Services: Email marketing platforms to send order confirmations and newsletters
  • Analytics Services: Tools to analyze website traffic and improve user experience

These service providers have access to your information only to perform specific tasks on our behalf and are contractually obligated to protect your data.

Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law or to protect our rights:

  • Comply with legal obligations, court orders, or government requests
  • Protect our rights, property, or safety, or that of our users
  • Prevent fraud or enforce our terms of service
  • Respond to legal claims or investigations

Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred to the new owner. We will notify you of any such change in control.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information:

Encryption

All sensitive data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted using SSL/TLS encryption. Payment information is encrypted during transmission and processed through secure payment gateways that comply with PCI DSS standards.

Data Storage

We store your data in secure hosting environments with restricted access. Passwords are stored using strong hashing algorithms. We do not store complete credit card numbers on our servers.

Access Controls

Access to your personal information is limited to authorized personnel who need it to perform their job functions. All employees undergo privacy training and are bound by confidentiality agreements.

Regular Security Reviews

We regularly review our security practices and update them as necessary to address new threats and vulnerabilities. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience and analyze website traffic.

Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly, including maintaining your login session, storing items in your cart, and processing orders.

Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors use our website, which pages are popular, and how we can improve the user experience. The data collected is anonymized and aggregated.

Marketing Cookies

With your consent, we may use marketing cookies to show you personalized advertisements and track the effectiveness of our marketing campaigns.

Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling essential cookies may prevent you from using certain features of our website, such as adding items to your cart or completing a purchase.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information:

Access and Correction

You have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you and to request correction of any inaccurate or incomplete information.

Data Deletion

You may request the deletion of your personal information, subject to certain legal and business requirements. We will retain necessary information to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

Data Portability

You can request a copy of your personal information in a structured, commonly used format.

Opt-Out of Marketing

You can opt out of receiving marketing communications by clicking the unsubscribe link in our emails or updating your account preferences.

Account Closure

You may request the closure of your account at any time. We will retain necessary information for legal and business purposes but will delete your account from active use.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@gedaaistar.et.

7. International Data Transfers

Gadaa Istar is based in Ethiopia and operates internationally. Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence. We ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

Export Compliance

As an exporter of Ethiopian products, we comply with Ethiopian data protection laws and international data transfer regulations when processing customer information for international orders.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us, and we will delete such information.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy on our website and updating the "Last updated" date.

Your continued use of our services after any changes to this Privacy Policy constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
