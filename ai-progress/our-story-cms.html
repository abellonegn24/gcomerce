Our story

Four sister companies.
One Ethiopian vision.

Gadaa Istar Business Group Share Company is a conglomerate business uniting four sister companies — G-Plus, G-Jila, G-Farm, and G-Empire — under one management team headquartered in Addis Ababa. With a combined start-up investment of 28 billion Birr, an anticipated ETB 64.7 billion in annual net profit, and roughly 100,000 new jobs on the horizon, we're building East Africa's next industrial giant — one shipment, one hire, and one community at a time.
